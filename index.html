Devon Avenue, delivered.

Devon Avenue is Chicago's South Asian food street — and ordering from it online usually means a third-party app taking a heavy cut from a family kitchen. Order Devon is a single ordering hub being built with the avenue's restaurants, on terms a small kitchen can actually live with. Why one street gets its own app

Built for the avenue, not above it.

Flat fees, published openly

One flat per-order fee, printed on this site before launch. No percentage commissions that grow with a kitchen's success, and no charging diners hidden markups.

Kitchens keep their customers

Order history and regulars belong to the restaurant, not to us. A kitchen can export its own customer list any day, or leave with it any time.

One neighborhood, done right

We're not trying to cover a city. One corridor, every kitchen on it, pickup-first with delivery close to home — the radius a fresh naan survives.

For restaurants

Be a founding kitchen.

Who this is for: any restaurant, counter, or sweet shop on or around Devon Avenue that wants online ordering without handing a percentage of every order to a platform.

What founding kitchens get: first position in the launch lineup, founding terms locked permanently, menu and photo setup done with you, and a direct say in how ordering, pickup, and delivery work before anything ships.

What it costs to join: nothing before launch. The flat per-order fee will be published on this page before any diner places an order.
